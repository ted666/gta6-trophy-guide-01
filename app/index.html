    Roadmap to the platinum

    Around 200 hours in total, 4/10 difficulty (PowerPyx). One playthrough, no online trophies, free roam after the story. Do it in three stages — story first, then the 100% checklist, then character quests and the five unknown trophies.

    Before you start

    • Keep a save at the start and end of every chapter (10 saves). The game gives you 15 manual save slots plus 1 autosave — rotate the other 5 (PowerPyx).
    • The one missable trophy is Positive Mental Attitude. Meditate with Lucia on Jason’s balcony in Chapter 1, after “Down to Earth”. After Chapter 1 it is gone for good.
    • Cheats disable trophies and saving. There are 61 cheats; none work before the credits, and the block lasts for the current session (GTABase via PowerPyx).
    • No mission replay. You cannot replay a story mission, so finish side tasks as you go — but free roam after the story covers almost everything.
    • Turn on Criminal Profile notifications (Settings > UI > HUD Notifications > Criminal Profile = On) so Actions Speak Louder progress is visible.

    Missable trophies

    Only one trophy in the whole list can be missed. Everything else stays available in free roam after the story.

    Not missable — but don’t panic

    • Kenton (shipwrecks) goes quiet after Chapter 1, but returns in Chapters 2–3.
    • Kingpin Bowling valuables respawn — if you miss one, go back later.
    • Chapter trophies pop after the first mission of the next chapter — that is normal, not a bug.
    • Andy, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret questlines are all still open after the story.
